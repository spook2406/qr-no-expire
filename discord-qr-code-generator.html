How Discord invite QR codes work

Discord invite links usually look like https://discord.gg/CODE or https://discord.com/invite/CODE (older links may use discordapp.com/invite). This page normalizes those hosts to https://discord.gg/CODE, strips tracking query noise, and encodes the clean URL into a static QR.

Scanning opens Discord (app or web) for that invite. Whether someone can join still depends on Discord and whether the invite remains valid.

Never-expire / unlimited uses (important for print)

Discord invites often expire—commonly after 7 days—and may have a maximum number of uses. A static QR printed on a poster will keep encoding the same URL forever on our side, but if Discord revokes the invite, lets it expire, or hits the use cap, scanners will fail.

For a printed QR that should keep working: in Discord, create an invite set to Never expire and No limit / unlimited uses, then paste that URL here. If you later revoke the invite or change server settings, regenerate the code.

FAQ

Do Discord invite QR codes expire?

Often yes—on Discord’s side. Default invites commonly expire (for example after 7 days) and may have a max-use limit. For print, use Never expire + unlimited uses. If the invite is revoked, expires, or hits its use cap, the static QR stops working. We do not host a short link—the QR just encodes the URL you pasted.

Does this Discord QR expire on our side?

A static payload does not expire because of a generator trial—the URL lives in the image. Discord still controls invite validity.

What links are accepted?

discord.gg/CODE, discord.com/invite/CODE, and discordapp.com/invite/CODE (http/https/www). Channel deep links like discord.com/channels/… and login/OAuth URLs are rejected.
