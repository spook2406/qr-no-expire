How Zoom meeting QR codes work

Zoom join links usually look like https://zoom.us/j/{meetingId} or a regional host such as https://us02web.zoom.us/j/{meetingId}. Many invites also include a password query: ?pwd=…. This page keeps the host and path, keeps pwd, strips tracking params, and encodes the clean HTTPS URL into a static QR.

Scanning opens Zoom (app or web) for that meeting. Whether someone can join still depends on Zoom and whether the meeting / password remain valid. Waiting rooms and host authentication still apply—encoding the URL does not bypass them.

Password links: do not post publicly in uncontrolled places

If your join URL includes ?pwd=, anyone who scans the QR can join with that password. Do not publicly post that QR or link on open social feeds or random public pages. Use it for invited attendees or controlled spaces (office poster, invite email, private group).

FAQ

Do Zoom meeting QR codes expire?

If the meeting is deleted, ends as a scheduled event that is no longer joinable, or the password changes, the static QR stops working. We do not host a short link—the QR just encodes the URL you pasted. The QR itself does not expire on our side.

Does this Zoom QR expire on our side?

A static payload does not expire because of a generator trial—the URL lives in the image. Zoom still controls whether the meeting remains joinable.

Is the meeting password kept?

Yes. When ?pwd= is present, we keep it on the encoded URL. Tracking params like utm_*, fbclid, and gclid are stripped; pwd is never stripped.

Is this an official Zoom tool?

No. QRStatic is not affiliated with Zoom Video Communications. Zoom® is a trademark of its respective owners.

Is my join URL uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser. We do not upload URLs or mint dynamic short links. No signup, watermark, or trial.
