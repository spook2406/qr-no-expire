Contact us

Questions about the free static QR generator on qrstatic.app, a bug report, privacy request, or partnership note — we read every message.

Email

Write to smithk@gmail.com.

Please include:

  • What you were trying to do (URL / WiFi / vCard / calendar / Google review QR, or a guide page)
  • Your browser and device (for example Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone)
  • Screenshots or the exact error text, if something failed

We aim to reply within a few business days. There is no phone line and no chat widget — email keeps your request in one place and avoids collecting numbers we do not need.

GitHub

For reproducible bugs or documentation fixes, open an issue on the public repository: github.com/spook2406/qr-no-expire.

Privacy

QR payloads are generated in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers. Analytics and ads disclosures live on the Privacy Policy. For a privacy-related request, email the same address and put “Privacy” in the subject line.
