How Spotify QR codes work

Spotify Share links usually look like https://open.spotify.com/playlist/{id}?si=…. The si (and similar) query values are attribution / tracking—handy for Spotify analytics, unnecessary on a printed poster. This page keeps https://open.spotify.com/{playlist|album|track|artist}/{id} and drops the query string.

Locale prefixes such as /intl-en/ are removed so the canonical path is stable across regions. spotify.link short URLs are accepted as-is with a warning—prefer the open.spotify.com canonical when you can copy it from the app.

Scanning opens Spotify (app or web) for that public resource. Availability still depends on the listener’s market, account, and whether the link remains public.

FAQ

What is a Spotify playlist QR code?

A static QR that encodes a public open.spotify.com playlist, album, track, or artist URL so phones can open Spotify when scanned.

Do you strip the ?si= tracking parameter?

Yes. We normalize to path-only https://open.spotify.com/{type}/{id} and drop si, utm_*, context, nd, and other query noise.

Is this an official Spotify tool?

No. QRStatic is not affiliated with Spotify AB. Spotify® is a trademark of Spotify AB.

Public vs private playlists?

Only share links you are allowed to make public. Private or collaborative links may not open for guests. Do not use this page to distribute unauthorized content.

Is my link uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser. We do not upload URLs or mint dynamic short links.

Does this Spotify QR expire?

A static payload does not expire because of a generator trial—the URL lives in the image. Spotify still controls playback rights and link availability.

What about spotify.link?

Accepted with a warning. For clean stripping and type detection, open the share sheet and copy the open.spotify.com URL instead.
