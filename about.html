About QRNoExpire

QRNoExpire helps people create static QR codes that never expire — without forcing an account or uploading data to a server.

We focus on the long-tail need for permanent, no-signup QR generation: URLs, text, WiFi, email, and phone payloads, with PNG and SVG downloads.

Technology

QR rendering uses the MIT-licensed soldair/node-qrcode library, bundled for the browser. Generation runs entirely on your device.
