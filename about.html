About qrstatic

qrstatic (qrstatic.app) is a free set of static QR tools. Codes are generated in your browser. There is no signup, and a static image does not depend on a subscription to keep working.

The payload—a URL, plain text, or a structured string such as Wi‑Fi or vCard—is written into the PNG or SVG you download. The site does not run a redirect that can expire the code later.

Tools

  • URL and text on the homepage generator
  • Wi‑Fi
  • vCard
  • Calendar
  • Google Review
  • SMS
  • WhatsApp
  • Email
  • Phone
  • Location
  • TOTP

Practical write-ups are in the guides.

Technology

QR rendering uses the MIT-licensed soldair/node-qrcode library, bundled for the browser. Generation runs entirely on your device.
