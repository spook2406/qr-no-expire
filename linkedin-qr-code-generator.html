Which links are accepted

The host, after www is removed, must be linkedin.com or a single-label subdomain such as uk.linkedin.com. Country subdomains are rewritten to linkedin.com so the printed code stays stable. http becomes https. The hash is dropped. Tracking parameters such as utm_*, fbclid, and gclid are removed. The public path stays:

  • https://linkedin.com/in/your-name — a public profile
  • https://linkedin.com/company/your-company — a public company page
  • https://linkedin.com/school/your-school — a public school page

You may omit https://. These examples are placeholders, not real pages.

What is rejected

Empty input is an error. Login, signup, authwall, checkpoint, and /uas/login paths are rejected. Share interstitial pages — share-offsite, sharing, shareArticle, and similar share steps — are rejected. lnkd.in, other shorteners, and any short link or redirect on qrstatic.app are rejected, because this page does not follow redirects. Other websites are rejected. None of those are silently encoded.

A scan does not make a private profile public. If the page is limited to signed-in members, LinkedIn still enforces that. This tool does not bypass visibility settings.
