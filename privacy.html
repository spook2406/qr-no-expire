Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026 (Asia/Shanghai)

What we process

QR payloads (URLs, text, WiFi credentials, email, phone) are processed only in your browser. They are not sent to our servers because this site is a static GitHub Pages site with no backend API for generation.

Hosting & logs

Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard web server / CDN logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) according to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Cookies & ads

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-JV95W19XS3). Page views and basic usage may be collected by Google, which may set first-party analytics cookies. See Google’s privacy policy.

QR payloads (URLs, text, WiFi credentials, email, phone) stay in your browser. They are not uploaded to us.

Google AdSense may load third-party scripts and cookies for ads (publisher ID ca-pub-8737820277849030). You can contact us at smithk@gmail.com about privacy questions.

Children

The service is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: email smithk@gmail.com or open an issue on the project repository.
