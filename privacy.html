Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 (Asia/Shanghai)

This policy covers qrstatic.app, a set of free static QR tools. There is no signup.

What we process

QR payloads (URLs, text, Wi‑Fi credentials, email, phone, review links, and the other generator fields) are processed only in your browser. They are not sent to our servers because this site is a static GitHub Pages site with no backend API for generation.

How generation works on your device

When you choose Generate, JavaScript on the page builds a text payload and passes it to a copy of the MIT-licensed node-qrcode library running in your browser. The library draws the modules on a canvas in the page. Download PNG or Download SVG asks your browser to save that drawing as a file on your device. The form is not submitted with a POST, and there is no qrstatic.app endpoint that accepts the fields.

Examples of payloads built this way: a URL or plain text; a WIFI: connection string; a mailto: or tel: URI; a vCard or calendar block; an SMSTO: draft; a https://wa.me/ link; a Google review URL; a Maps URL or geo: pair; an otpauth:// authenticator URI; and the public Spotify, Telegram, Discord, Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams link you pasted. The exact string is visible in the page after you generate (preview, and on most tools a copy or payload readout). It is not transmitted to us as part of generation.

We do not keep a history of codes you created, and we cannot retrieve a payload later. If you email the PNG to someone, upload it, or print it, that copy is under your control and can be scanned by anyone who can see it. A Wi‑Fi password or a TOTP secret inside an image should be treated as a printed secret.

Hosting & logs

Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard web server / CDN logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) according to GitHub’s privacy statement. How long those logs are kept is determined by GitHub, not by this site.

Cookies & ads

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-JV95W19XS3). Page views and basic usage may be collected by Google, which may set first-party analytics cookies. See Google’s privacy policy.

Our generator code does not upload QR payloads to qrstatic.app and does not attach them to an analytics event. They are not stored in an account, because there is no account. Google Analytics and Google AdSense are third-party scripts that run in the same page. Any script loaded by the page can read the document the way other JavaScript can. If you are typing a Wi‑Fi password, a TOTP secret, or another credential, that is a reason to treat the page like any page that loads third-party code: do not leave those values on a shared computer, and do not assume a third-party script is incapable of seeing the form.

Google AdSense may load scripts and cookies for ads (publisher ID ca-pub-8737820277849030), including cookies used to measure or personalize ads. The homepage loads the AdSense script for that publisher. You can review ad personalization in Google Ads Settings. You can contact us at smithk@gmail.com about privacy questions.

Children

The service is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.
