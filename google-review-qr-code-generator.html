FAQ

  
Is this an official Google review QR tool?

  
No. It is a free static QR generator. You supply a Google link or Place ID you already have.


  
Do Google review QR codes expire?

  
A static encode of your review URL does not expire because a QR-tool trial ended. Retest if Google changes link formats.


  
What if I paste a random website?

  
Non-Google URLs show a clear error and do not silently generate a misleading code.


  
Is my link uploaded?

  
No. Client-side only—same privacy model as WiFi and vCard.
