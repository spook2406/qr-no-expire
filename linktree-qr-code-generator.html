Which links are accepted

The host must be linktr.ee or linktr.biz after a single www. label is removed. http becomes https. The hash is dropped. One trailing slash is dropped. Tracking and affiliate parameters, including utm*, fbclid, and gclid, are removed. This page does not add any affiliate or campaign parameter of its own. The username is one path segment, kept as pasted: letter case is not changed, the name is not completed, and nothing is sent over the network to check that the name exists. The public homepage stays:

  • https://linktr.ee/yourusername — a public homepage on linktr.ee
  • https://linktr.biz/yourusername — the same shape on linktr.biz

You may omit https://. These examples are shapes, not real pages. MyName stays MyName.

What is rejected

Empty input is an error. Login, register, logout, and forgot-password flows are rejected. Admin, Account, Settings, Insights, Billing, upgrade, and subscribe are rejected. Help and marketing paths are rejected, including help, blog, pricing, about, legal, privacy, terms, explore, discover, jobs, api, shop, store, cards, invite, and connect. The same reserved paths are rejected on both linktr.ee and linktr.biz. This page does not bypass a login wall.

linktree.com is rejected. That root is not a user homepage. Hosts other than the two public homepage hosts are rejected, including cards.linktr.ee, ciam.linktr.ee, and ugc.production.linktr.ee. A custom domain is rejected, because this page does not follow a network redirect or check who owns the domain. A short link or an unknown unique URL is rejected and is not unwrapped. A path with more than one segment, such as /{username}/… or a help article, is rejected. Other link-in-bio tools, including Beacons and Later, are rejected. A short link or redirect on qrstatic.app is rejected, and the QR never contains qrstatic.app. None of those are silently encoded.

Print the code

Keep the quiet zone, dark modules on a light background, and do not overlay the official Linktree logo in the center of the code. The quiet zone is the empty margin around the modules; phones need that margin to find the code. Linktree’s help says to make the QR code’s color darker than its background so it scans reliably, and to test a scan with a phone before you print. Leave the default black modules on a white background unless you have checked a sample print. Download SVG when a printer will scale the mark, and PNG for a screen proof.
