Static vs dynamic QR codes

Updated for 2026

Static QR

  • Payload is inside the image
  • Works offline after print (for text/WiFi/tel; URLs still need the site online)
  • No vendor account required
  • Cannot change destination without reprinting
  • Best for: WiFi cards, permanent product packaging, personal contact, evergreen pages

Dynamic QR

  • Printed code points to a short redirect URL
  • Destination can be edited later
  • Often includes scan analytics
  • Depends on the redirect service staying available and affordable
  • Best for: campaigns, events, menus that change weekly — when you pay for reliability

Our recommendation

If you searched for a static QR code with no signup or a QR code generator free no expiration, start static. Keep dynamic for paid, intentional campaign use.
