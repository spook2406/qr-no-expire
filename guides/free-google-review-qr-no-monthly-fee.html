A paid dynamic review QR makes sense only when you must change the destination without reprinting, such as frequent campaign swaps you will keep paying to host, while one location with a stable Google review URL is better served by a static encode that has no monthly fee and no trial clock inside the image.

  • A static file cannot be retargeted from a dashboard. A new URL means a new PNG or SVG.
  • Most single-location shops with one official review link do not need that redirect.
