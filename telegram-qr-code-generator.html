How Telegram QR codes work

Public Telegram share links usually look like https://t.me/username, https://t.me/+InviteHash, or the older https://t.me/joinchat/… form. This page normalizes telegram.me → t.me, @user → https://t.me/user, and joinchat → the modern https://t.me/+… form when possible, then drops tracking query noise.

Scanning opens Telegram (app or web) for that public username, group, channel, or invite. Availability still depends on Telegram and whether the invite remains valid.

FAQ

Is this a Telegram login or device-authorization QR?

No. Public t.me / @username / invite links only. Login-like tg://login payloads are rejected.

What if an invite is revoked or expires?

The static QR still contains the same URL, but Telegram will reject a revoked or expired invite. We do not host a short link—the QR just encodes the URL. Create a fresh invite you are allowed to share and regenerate the code.

Does this Telegram QR expire on our side?

A static payload does not expire because of a generator trial—the URL lives in the image. Telegram still controls usernames, groups, channels, and invite validity.

Is this an official Telegram tool?

No. QRStatic is not affiliated with Telegram Messenger Inc. Telegram® is a trademark of its respective owners.

Can I use this for unauthorized invites or ban evasion?

No. Only paste public links you have the right to share. Do not use this tool to help unauthorized recruiting or to bypass Telegram enforcement.

Is my link uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser. We do not upload URLs or mint dynamic short links. No signup, watermark, or trial.
